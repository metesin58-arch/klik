Frequently asked questions

Where are my photos saved?

Every shot is kept in the app's own roll. Open a photo and tap Save to copy it to your Photos library, or Share to send it.

The camera screen is black.

KLIK needs camera access. Go to Settings > KLIK and turn on Camera, then reopen the app.

Saving to Photos does not work.

Go to Settings > KLIK > Photos and choose "Add Photos Only" or "Full Access".

How do I change the language?

KLIK follows your device language. You can also switch between English and Turkish in the app's settings.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open the KLIK Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Does KLIK collect my data?

No. Photos are processed on your device and are never uploaded. See our Privacy Policy.
